🛡️ Scout Rules

Before recommending an opportunity, the Manager checks eligibility, requirements, payout, minimum withdrawal, payment method, legitimacy signals, automation permission, expected net hourly rate, repeatability and whether completion/payout can be verified automatically.

Discovery is broader than the seed list: the Manager may research microtasks, data-labeling, transcription, testing, research/data collection, content production, affiliate/referral work, digital services, open paid APIs/data work and other legitimate zero-upfront-cost paths. Seed platforms are examples, not the boundary of the system.

Never: bypass CAPTCHA, create fake identities/accounts, fake reviews or engagement, evade restrictions, or move money without approval.
